# Pitch deck outline (10 slides)

1. **Larea.** Talk to the people right where you are. One line, the icon, the two team names.
2. **The problem.** The room you're standing in has no channel. Online communities are global and anonymous; the people within 50 metres are unreachable. A photo of a full café.
3. **The product.** One chat per real place. Join only within 200 m. 18+ only. AI moderation before anyone sees a message. Three phone screenshots: map, chat, members.
4. **Why Solana Mobile.** On a Seeker, presence becomes something you own. Check in → soulbound stamp → the chat opens. The stamp is the key. Screenshot of the wallet approving a stamp mint.
5. **The loop.** Stamps → levels → badge, Regulars room, SKR rewards → places reward people who show up. A simple diagram: Visitor → Regular → Local → Legend.
6. **Money in the room.** Tips in USDC and SKR wallet to wallet. A neighbourhood market settled in USDC through escrow with a handover code. Screenshot of a tip in the chat.
7. **How it's built.** Native Kotlin and Compose. Mobile Wallet Adapter and Sign In With Solana. Backend builds every transaction (umi, Bubblegum V2, mpl-core), verifies the message hash before and after signing. Starter funds on devnet. Architecture sketch.
8. **Stickiness.** Daily habits: a stamp per place per day, levels that take weeks, rooms that only regulars can enter, perks run by the place. Why someone opens it every day.
9. **Status and what's next.** Live on devnet; APK, website, repo links. Next: dApp Store listing, mainnet with real USDC and SKR, App Attest and Play Integrity hardening, venue-run perks.
10. **The team and the ask.** Two builders in Germany. What we want from Solana Mobile: dApp Store featuring and Seekers in the hands of regulars at five Berlin places.

Design notes: dark background, one idea per slide, real screenshots over mock-ups, numbers only where they mean something (200 m, 24 h, one stamp per day). Export as PDF.
