# Demo video: storyboard and narration (2:30)

Record on the teammate's phone with a real wallet on devnet, against the demo server. Screen-record the phone (Android's built-in recorder) and film a few seconds of the phone in the real place with a second phone. Read the narration over it afterwards. Keep every shot short; judges score completion from this video.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Street-level shot of a café or library entrance, then the Larea icon. | "Every real place is full of people with the same questions and no way to ask. Larea gives every place its own chat, and you can only join while you're standing there." |
| 0:12–0:30 | Sign up, the 18+ step, location permission. Speed up 2×. | "Everyone is an adult, every message is checked by an AI moderator before anyone sees it, and your exact position never leaves your phone unverified." |
| 0:30–0:50 | Profile → Connect wallet → Seeker wallet or Phantom sign-in sheet → back in Larea, the welcome gift card and balances. | "On Solana Mobile, your wallet is your identity. One tap signs in with Sign In With Solana. The app never holds a key." |
| 0:50–1:20 | Nearby map with real places → tap the place → Check in & join → wallet approves → "Stamp collected" → the chat opens. Cut to Profile → My stamps and the explorer page of the mint. | "Here's the idea: checking in mints a soulbound stamp into your wallet. The stamp is what opens the chat. Not a password. Proof you were there." |
| 1:20–1:40 | Chat: send a message, a photo, start a poll. Long-press a message → Tip 2 USDC → wallet approves → the "tipped" line appears in the room. | "Inside, it's a normal chat. Except you can tip the person who helped you, wallet to wallet, in USDC or SKR." |
| 1:40–2:00 | Levels: Profile shows Regular at this place with the badge; the SKR reward line; the Regulars room in the chat. | "Come back and your stamps add up. Regulars get a badge, their own room, and SKR rewards. Places get to know who actually shows up." |
| 2:00–2:15 | Market: a listing priced in USDC; accept an offer; the escrow payment; the handover code screen. | "The neighbourhood market settles in USDC through escrow, released with a code when you meet." |
| 2:15–2:30 | Explorer tab with the devnet transactions, then the Larea wordmark and the website URL. | "Native Android, Mobile Wallet Adapter, compressed NFTs, all on devnet today. Larea: be there." |

Checklist before recording:

- Demo server up with Solana on, the phone's wallet on devnet, "Install unknown apps" allowed.
- Use a fresh account so the welcome gift and the first stamp appear on camera.
- Have one teammate already in the chat from a second phone so a reply and a tip have a recipient.
- Record in portrait; export at 1080p; upload to YouTube unlisted and to Drive.
